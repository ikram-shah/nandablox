# Backlog

Ideas from the Peter and Vedh sessions of 2026-11-05 that aren't built yet.
The verbatim quotes and transcripts are in the private notes repo
(`mit-nanda/notes/2026-11-05.md`).

## Historical settings

Same game (agents, monitors, Nandabux, the 5-minute round) on different maps,
each with its own goods, costumes and frauds.

> **Vedh:** what if we try to simulate, like, the Silk Road or something? …
> Or like the Portuguese, right? And the spice trade or something. Like, you're
> actually, like, you know, using the caravels and stuff. … We're actually
> doing the sailing. … you take these very historical, like, time periods where
> there was a lot of exchange, um, and you just bring agents into it.

- [ ] **Medieval marketplace**, themed by region (China, India, Europe, South America), with local goods.
- [ ] **The Silk Road**: caravans and middlemen; long supply chains.
- [ ] **The Portuguese spice trade, with caravels**: crewed sailing becomes literal, a crew on a ship.
- [ ] **Modern-day San Francisco** (fentanyl), "if you're really gutsy".
- [ ] **Characters in real costumes**, not NPC-ish rigs.

## Red

- [ ] **Fake buyers** who scam sellers (fake coin, "never arrived").
- [ ] **Decoy monitors**: Red registers a monitor that Blue mistakes for its own.
- [ ] **Decoy buyers**: a Red player poses as an ordinary shopper; shirts aren't proof of team.
- [ ] **Undercover blue**: a Red player on the watch team blocks inspections of Red's stalls.

## Blue

- [ ] **Undercover red**: a Blue player gets a scammer to admit a scam, then acts on the evidence.
- [ ] **Tamper-evident heuristics**, so decoy monitors and undercover blues leave traces.
- [ ] **Verified, immutable proof** of each legitimate trade; Blue scores on a quota of them.

## Governance

- [ ] **Councils on both sides**: a small enforcement council inside Blue, a "council of two" on Red.
- [ ] **Coalitions across teams**, and leaders and dissent within a team.
- [ ] **Legalised, taxed markets that crime still runs**: a scenario where Red and Blue cooperate.

## Demo and audience

- [ ] **QR code join**: random team assignment; Blue's first 30 seconds is an audience vote on the loadout.
- [ ] **Agents entering from all directions** when a wave arrives (the zoom-out itself is built).
- [ ] **Outside overseer**: an LLM watching both teams and writing a report.
- [ ] **`skill.md`** in the starter kit, for defining a custom agent's skills.
- [ ] **Enterprise onboarding**: companies drop their own services into the town to see how they hold up.
